# Chat we are so back!

It's been a long quiet stretch, but Senpwai is back with its biggest overhaul
yet. The app is rebuilt in Flutter, is now available on Android, Linux, macOS,
Windows, and packed in a lot more than a fresh coat of paint. This is the first
beta of Senpwai 3! 🥳

- **Find your next anime:** Discover and search through AniList, filter the
  results, and connect your account to browse your library.
- **Pick how you download:** Grab episodes from Animepahe, Tokyo Insider, or
  Nyaa. Choose your episode range, resolution, audio
  preference, source, and download folder.
- **Let Senpwai do the tedious bits:** Scan folders to skip episodes you already
  have, optionally skip filler, and review Nyaa matches before a torrent batch
  starts.
- **Stay in control:** Pause, resume, and reorder downloads while watching
  progress, speed, and torrent activity in one queue.
- **Never miss the next episode:** Track ongoing anime and let Senpwai check for
  new episodes using the download choices you saved for each show.
- **Make it yours:** Switch themes, typography, and card layouts in an interface
  built for phones, tablets, and desktops. ✨

This is a beta, so expect and report bugs. Windows
installers are unsigned and the macOS app is ad-hoc signed; your system may ask
you to approve the first launch.

Believers rise up, the doubters are in shambles!
