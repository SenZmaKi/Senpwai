# Senpwai 3.0.1: Nothing Happened

Senpwai’s first stable v3 update makes downloads easier to resume and gives you clearer guidance when a folder, audio track, or source is unavailable. Thanks to everyone who sent in bug reports after 3.0.0!

- **Download queue:** Resume requests are less likely to time out while waiting for another download operation. Senpwai now also blocks duplicate entries for the same torrent file, even when you pick a different download folder.
- **Download folders:** If Senpwai can’t read your chosen folder, it tells you to pick one it can access.
- **AnimePahe:** An unreadable download option no longer prevents Senpwai from showing the other options for that episode.
- **AnimeHeaven:** Downloads and tracking show an error if you try to pair AnimeHeaven with English audio. Your previous selection stays in place.
- **AniList:** Search shows a clearer message when AniList is temporarily unavailable.
- **Desktop:** Fixed an error that could occur when closing Senpwai. On Windows, browser sessions can now reset even when cookie cleanup fails.

You may also see a one-time invitation from the Senpwai mascot to star the project on GitHub. A star helps more people find the app. ⭐
