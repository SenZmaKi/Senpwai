# The One Piece Is Real!

Senpwai 3.0.0 is here! After three betas, the Flutter rebuild graduates to its first stable release on Android, Linux, macOS, and Windows. Thank you to everyone who tried it, reported bugs, and helped bring Senpwai back. 🥳

**Find your next anime:** Discover and search through AniList, filter results, and connect your account to browse your library.

**Download your way:** Choose AnimeHeaven, Nyaa, AnimePahe, or Tokyo Insider, with your preferred episode range, resolution, audio language, and folder. Scan for episodes you already have and review uncertain torrent matches before downloading.

**Keep up automatically:** Track ongoing anime with separate source, quality, language, folder, and filler preferences for each show. Episode badges make filler easier to spot.

**Pick up where you left off:** Your queue, paused downloads, and torrent seeding progress survive restarts. Interrupted transfers resume where supported; some sources require fresh links or a restart.

**Make it yours:** Customize themes, typography, cards, browsing details, and animations on phones, tablets, and desktops. ✨

**Better bug reports:** Copy diagnostics or send an error report to Discord from the app.

**Already on a v3 beta?** Update to stable through Senpwai's updater without uninstalling. Your settings, tracked anime, and download queue stay with you.

Windows installers remain unsigned and the macOS app is ad hoc signed, so your system may ask you to approve the first launch.

Believers rise up. Senpwai is back!
